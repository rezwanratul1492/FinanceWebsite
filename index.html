01 · ABOUT · BANGLADESH → UNITED STATES

I keep coming back to one question:
Why?

I am originally from Bangladesh, and my path into finance began during my undergraduate studies at North South University. I was drawn to finance because of the analytical way it helps us make sense of decisions. Over time, I realized that I enjoyed not only learning those ideas, but also helping others understand them—and that gradually led me toward academia.

WHY?

In teaching, I often begin with that question. I believe understanding why something works gives us a stronger foundation for understanding everything that follows.
